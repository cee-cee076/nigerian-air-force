Our Mission

Air power in service of the nation

The Nigerian Air Force exists to employ air power in defence of Nigeria's territorial integrity, in aid of civil authority, and in support of regional and global peace operations.

  • Defend Nigerian airspaceAir policing and quick-reaction alert from bases across the federation.
  • Support joint operationsClose air support, interdiction and ISR alongside the Army and Navy.
  • Aid to civil authorityDisaster relief, casualty evacuation and humanitarian airlift.
