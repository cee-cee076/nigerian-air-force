Entry Routes

Three ways in

OFFICER CADET

NDA Regular Course

Earn a degree at the Nigerian Defence Academy, Kaduna, and commission as a Pilot Officer.

  • School-leavers with credits in core subjects
  • Five-year academic and military programme
  • Pathway to pilot and specialist branches
Check eligibility
GRADUATES
DIRECT ENTRY

Direct Short Service Commission

For graduates and professionals (engineers, doctors, lawyers, ICT specialists and more) who want to serve as commissioned officers.

  • First degree or HND, plus NYSC
  • Military training at the Military School
  • Commission into your professional branch
Check eligibility
AIRMEN / AIRWOMEN

Basic Military Training

Enlist as an Aircraftman or Aircraftwoman and train in a trade: technical, regiment, medical, admin and more.

  • SSCE/NECO holders and tradesmen
  • Basic training, then trade training
  • Progress to NCO and warrant ranks
Check eligibility

FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to pay to apply?

No. NAF recruitment is free at every stage. Report anyone asking for money to the nearest NAF unit or the police.

Can women join the NAF?

Yes. Women serve as officers and airwomen across many branches, including as pilots.

When does recruitment open?

Recruitment exercises are announced on the official NAF website and portal and through official social media channels.

Can I become a pilot through Direct Short Service?

Pilot entry is usually through the NDA cadet route or specific direct-entry calls. Check each year's advertised requirements.
