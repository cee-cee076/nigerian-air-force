FAQ

Before you write

How do I request an interview with a senior officer?

Send a media inquiry with your organisation, the topic and your deadline. The Directorate of Public Relations and Information handles all interview requests.

Can schools arrange a visit to an air base?

Educational visits are arranged through community relations, subject to security and operational requirements.

How do I verify official NAF social media accounts?

Use only the links published on airforce.mil.ng. Impersonation accounts are common, especially during recruitment.

Where can I get NAF photos for publication?

Request official imagery from the media desk. The photos in this site's gallery are freely licensed images from Wikimedia Commons.
