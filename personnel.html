Ranks & insignia

Reading the insignia

Stripes & chevrons

Officer insignia follow the Commonwealth air force pattern. Junior and field officers wear narrow stripes. Air officers, from Air Commodore upwards, wear a broad band plus extra stripes.

NCO ranks wear chevrons. Warrant officers wear the coat of arms of Nigeria.

The insignia drawn here are simplified for screen. Official dress regulations govern the exact designs.
