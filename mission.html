Mission Sets

What we deliver

Air Defence

Surveillance and policing of Nigerian airspace, with the Nigerian Air Defence Corps and quick-reaction fighters.

Close Air Support

Precision fires from fixed- and rotary-wing platforms in direct support of ground forces.

ISR

Persistent intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance from crewed aircraft and armed UAVs.

Air Mobility

Tactical airlift, airdrop and troop movement at home and on peace-support deployments.

Search & Rescue

Casualty evacuation and SAR, including medical evacuation for wounded personnel.

Humanitarian Aid

Disaster relief airlift, medical outreach and support to civil authorities in emergencies.
